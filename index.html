Custom work, built to order

From your idea or your old gold. Bring a sketch, a photo, or an inherited piece you'd like remade. We design around what you have and tell you plainly when melting down heirloom metal is — and isn't — worth it.

Approved before it's made. Every custom job gets a drawing and a fixed written quote before we touch metal. The price you approve is the price you pay.

Finished like it matters. Settings checked under magnification, stones tightened, and a final clean before handoff. If a prong we built fails in the first year, we re-tip it at no charge.

Repairs, with the estimate first

Most repairs are small jobs done well. You'll get a written estimate before any work starts, and your piece never leaves our bench.

Ring sizing

Up or down, plain bands to pavé shanks. Stones checked and re-seated after sizing.

Chain & clasp repair

Broken links rebuilt and soldered, worn clasps replaced with ones rated for the chain's weight.

Stone tightening & replacement

Loose stones secured; lost stones matched by cut, color, and size — matched honestly, documented plainly.

Prong re-tipping

Worn prongs rebuilt before they fail — the cheapest insurance a stone can have.

Watch batteries & bands

While-you-wait battery swaps and band adjustments on most models.

Polish & rhodium

Scratches worked out by hand; white gold re-plated so it reads white again, not gray.

Our rule on repairs: if a piece isn't worth fixing, we say so. We'd rather lose a small job than have you pay more than the piece is worth.
