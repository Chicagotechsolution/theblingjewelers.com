Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The Bling Jewelers is currently a static, pre-opening website and collects as little data as possible.

What we collect

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The appointment-request form does not transmit anywhere until the shop opens; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.

When the shop opens

Appointment booking will carry its own clear privacy terms, published before any data is collected.
